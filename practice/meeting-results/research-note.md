# Research note: Travis County, TX, business personal property, tax year 2026

FICTIONAL TRAINING CONTEXT, not tax advice. No practice amount changed. Checked 2026-09-06 by web fetch. Every finding stays Needs review until a reviewer signs off. Official sources only: TCAD, the Texas Comptroller, the Texas Legislature.

## R1. Rendition deadline stated by the sources

TCAD's renditions page states an April 15 deadline. Comptroller Form 50-144 (revision 03-26) says renditions are due after January 1 and by April 15, extendable to May 15 on written request, plus 15 days for good cause. Whether a property must render is not decided here.

- Source: https://traviscad.org/renditions (page dated 2026-06-29)
- Source: https://comptroller.texas.gov/forms/50-144.pdf (revision 03-26, Deadlines table)
- Status: Needs review. TCAD's page shows no tax year and no extension procedure.

## R2. Where to file

TCAD lists an online portal, mail to PO Box 141864, Austin, TX 78714-1864, and a drop box at 850 E Anderson Ln, Austin, TX 78752. Form 50-144 says to file with the appraisal district, not the Comptroller.

## R3. $125,000 exemption and election not to render (unresolved)

HB 9 (signed 2025-06-12, effective 2026-01-01 only if voters approved HJR 1) exempts $125,000 of income-producing tangible personal property per location and adds Tax Code 22.01(j-1) to (j-3). Under (j-3) an election not to render takes effect beginning with the tax year following the year it is filed, so a 2026 election would not by itself remove a 2026 obligation.

- Source: https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB00009F.htm
- Source: https://capitol.texas.gov/BillLookup/History.aspx?LegSess=89R&Bill=HB9
- Status: Needs review. TCAD's page omits the exemption; the official vote result was not checked.
