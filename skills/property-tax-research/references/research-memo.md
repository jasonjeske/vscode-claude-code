# Research memo template

Use only sections relevant to the request. Replace bracketed placeholders with approved neutral
facts or explicit unknowns. Keep detailed workplace evidence in its approved location.

## Answer and scope

- Question: [PRECISE ISSUE]
- Jurisdiction and authority: [STATE / LOCALITY / ASSESSING OR TAXING AUTHORITY]
- Property type, relevant facts, and fact sources: [FACTS / UNKNOWNS]
- Tax year and relevant event/valuation date: [YEAR / DATE]
- Research as of: [DATE]; access mode: [LIVE APPROVED SOURCES / SOURCE-LIMITED]
- Short answer: [SUPPORTED FINDING WITH PINPOINT CITATION / WHAT IS UNDETERMINED]

## Findings

| Jurisdiction / tax year | Issue | Rule and exceptions | Pinpoint source | Effective period / applicability | Fact mapping or interpretation | Evidence coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [SCOPE] | [ISSUE] | [RULE OR UNKNOWN] | [SOURCE ID, SECTION, LINK] | [PERIOD OR UNKNOWN] | [FACTS AND GAPS] | [STATUS] |

Coverage describes the evidence, not a final tax determination:

- SUPPORTED: inspected applicable authority supports the stated finding within the recorded scope.
- PARTIAL: some support exists, but an identified authority, applicability check, or fact is missing.
- UNRESOLVED: available evidence does not support an answer.
- CONFLICT: potentially applicable sources or facts disagree and the conflict remains unresolved.

For a deadline, add obligation, trigger, trigger evidence, counting/adjustment rule, method/timezone
where applicable, conditional calculation, and reviewer status. For an exemption, add requirement,
supporting fact/evidence, application/renewal condition, and missing evidence. Do not imply that
research coverage equals eligibility or approval.
