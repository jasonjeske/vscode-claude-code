# Exemption evidence project instructions

Review and merge into `CLAUDE.md` in a separate approved work project. This template organizes
evidence; it does not supply eligibility rules. Keep filled facts out of the public repository.

## Question and scope

Research [EXEMPTION QUESTION] for [PROPERTY TYPE], [STATE AND LOCAL JURISDICTION], [TAX YEAR],
and [RELEVANT EFFECTIVE/VALUATION DATE]. Entity and ownership/use facts: [APPROVED SOURCE LABELS].
Final decision-maker: [AUTHORIZED REVIEWER ROLE].

## Authority

Use current applicable statutes, regulations, official forms/instructions, and assessor guidance
in the hierarchy approved by [REVIEWER]. For each source record issuer, title, URL or approved
document reference, section/page, effective period, retrieval date, and applicability.
Do not assume a current webpage governs a prior year. Preserve conflicting authorities and stop
for review. Secondary sources are research leads, not proof of eligibility.

## Evidence matrix

One row per requirement: requirement, authoritative citation, relevant fact, fact source,
effective period, MET/NOT MET/UNKNOWN evidence status, unresolved question, reviewer role.
Evidence status is not an eligibility approval. Do not infer missing ownership, use, situs,
valuation, filing history, or deadlines. Track renewal and initial application separately.

## Output

Draft a sourced memo, evidence index, missing-items list, and questions for human review. Extracted
dates remain unverified until checked against the applicable rule, year, and local instructions.
No filing, payment, portal action, legal conclusion, or final exemption determination.

## Boundaries and teaching

Browse only with generic research terms under the approved policy. Do not send internal identifiers
or documents to public search. Approved inputs/output locations: [LABELS]. Source conflicts and
unknown facts block a definitive recommendation. Default teaching mode is Guided: explain one
term and show one citation check. Use only documented and approved tools.
