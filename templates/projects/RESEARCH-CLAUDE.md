## Method

Invoke `/property-tax-workbench:property-tax-research` when installed. Inspect the applicable primary authority, pinpoint
citations, historical versions, exceptions, and local implementation. Distinguish enacted law,
pending proposals, controlling decisions, guidance, and forms. Do not assume a current page governs
a historical year. Generic external searches only; no internal identifiers or confidential facts.

Return source findings, applicability, interpretation, conflicting authority, and missing facts
separately. Each issue has SUPPORTED/PARTIAL/UNRESOLVED/CONFLICT research coverage. Include issuer,
direct source reference, section/page, publication/version, effective period, and retrieval date.
Blocked access means source-limited research, not a guessed answer. Do not claim a case remains
good law if current treatment was not checked.

Calculate deadlines only from supported rules and documented trigger evidence. Continue independent
research when one conclusion is blocked. No final filing position, exemption determination, portal
action, payment, submission, or source-workpaper change. The authorized reviewer owns decisions.

## Teaching and done

Default Guided mode: explain one useful source check. Done means the bounded question has a
supported answer or explicit gaps, traceable citations, and a concrete next verification step.
