Visual direction: near-black navy #080E18 and deep midnight blue #101D33, saturated clean royal blue #438EF5, ice blue #86BCFF, crisp white. Dark luxurious matte surfaces with restrained blue light, precise contemporary sans-serif typography, superb clarity and contrast. Sharp deliberate composition, realistic materials and depth. No sepia, beige, parchment, serif fonts, orange, cartoon illustration, clip art, robots, people, brains, generic AI sparkles, fake logos, watermarks, or hacker imagery.
